# Electric Surfboard vs Fuel-Powered Surfboard: Which Is Better for Your Business?

## Introduction

Electric surfboards and fuel-powered surfboards can both create exciting high-speed water experiences, but they serve different business needs. A resort may prefer electric boards because they are easier to present as clean, premium and beginner-friendly. A rental operator may care about charging schedule, session time and staff handling. A distributor may want both electric and fuel-powered models to cover different customer groups. An advanced rider may prefer the strong thrust and longer riding style of a fuel-powered board.

The right choice depends on the buyer's application, customer profile, maintenance capability, local rules and operating environment. This article compares electric surfboards and fuel-powered surfboards from a B2B perspective, including speed, endurance, operation, maintenance, training, shipment inspection and product positioning.

## Quick Answer: Which One Should You Choose?

Choose an electric surfboard if your business needs a cleaner, easier-to-manage product for resorts, rentals, yacht clubs, lakes, bays and premium guest experiences. Choose a fuel-powered surfboard if your market focuses on advanced riders, strong thrust, longer high-speed sessions and performance-oriented customers. Distributors may benefit from offering both categories because they answer different buyer needs.

## What Is an Electric Surfboard?

An electric surfboard uses an electric power system, battery pack, controller and propulsion system to move on water without waves. It is suitable for lakes, bays, rivers and coastal areas where local rules allow operation. The rider controls speed and direction while balancing on the board.

Electric surfboards are popular in B2B projects because they are easy to explain and visually attractive. They also fit modern resort positioning because they do not require fuel handling during daily operation. Battery charging and waterproof inspection become the main management points.

## What Is a Fuel-Powered Surfboard?

A fuel-powered surfboard uses a small engine and fuel system to generate thrust. It is often selected by advanced riders or buyers who want a stronger mechanical riding experience. Fuel-powered boards can be attractive for performance markets, distributors and water adventure operators.

ZAIHAI's fuel-powered line includes:

- ZAIHAI P1 Pro Fuel-Powered Surfboard: 10.5 kW / 8700 rpm, 64 km/h max speed, two-stroke water-cooled engine, 110 cc displacement, 3.5 L fuel tank, 150 kg max load.

Fuel-powered boards can be powerful and exciting, but they require fuel handling, engine maintenance and more technical operating knowledge.

## Speed and Performance

Speed is often the first comparison buyers ask about, but it should not be the only deciding factor.

The ZAIHAI X1 Pro electric model lists a 0 to 61 km/h speed range. The X1 lists a 0 to 51 km/h speed range. These speeds are already strong enough for commercial demos and premium resort experiences.

The ZAIHAI P1 and P1 Pro fuel-powered boards list max speeds of 62 km/h and 64 km/h. This makes them attractive for advanced riders and markets that value performance. However, speed must be matched with rider skill, water area and staff control.

For business buyers, the better question is:

- Who will ride the board?
- How much training will they receive?
- Is the water area controlled?
- Is the goal premium guest experience or advanced performance?
- Will staff be able to manage the product safely?

If the buyer's customers are first-time tourists, electric models may be easier to introduce. If the buyer's customers are experienced riders, fuel-powered models may be more attractive.

## Endurance and Daily Scheduling

Electric board endurance depends on battery capacity, speed mode, rider weight and water condition. The X1 specification lists 60 to 80 minutes of endurance, while the X1 Pro lists about 45 minutes. The X1 Pro provides stronger performance, but commercial buyers should plan session length and battery rotation carefully.

Fuel-powered boards use a fuel tank rather than a battery. The ZAIHAI P1 and P1 Pro list a 3.5 L fuel tank. For performance use, buyers should plan fuel preparation, fuel ratio, refueling process and staff safety rules.

For rental businesses, both categories require operational planning:

- Electric: charging schedule, spare batteries, charger storage, connector inspection.
- Fuel-powered: fuel supply, mixing ratio, engine check, ventilation, refueling safety.

The better choice depends on which process the operator can manage more reliably.

## Maintenance and Staff Skills

### Electric Surfboard Maintenance

Electric surfboards need careful battery and waterproof system management. Staff should inspect the board before each session and after use. They should check the battery compartment, connectors, tail area, board surface and remote control response.

Typical electric maintenance focus:

- Battery installation and removal.
- Charger condition.
- Waterproof seal inspection.
- Connector cleanliness.
- Propulsion area check.
- Rinsing and drying process.
- Storage in a dry and protected area.

Electric models may be easier for resorts and rental staff to manage if training is clear and spare parts are available.

### Fuel-Powered Surfboard Maintenance

Fuel-powered surfboards require more mechanical knowledge. Staff must understand fuel type, fuel ratio, engine basics, cooling system and routine inspection. For ZAIHAI P1 and P1 Pro, the listed fuel is unleaded gasoline No.95 and above, with a 50:1 fuel ratio and two-stroke water-cooled engine.

Typical fuel-powered maintenance focus:

- Correct fuel preparation.
- Engine start and response.
- Cooling system check.
- Fuel tank and line inspection.
- Tail and propulsion area check.
- Cleaning after riding.
- Storage with fuel safety considerations.

Fuel-powered boards may be better for buyers with technical staff or customers who already understand performance water equipment.

## Which Is Better for Resorts?

For most resorts, electric surfboards are usually easier to position. They match a premium, clean and modern guest experience. They also avoid daily fuel handling in front of guests. Staff can build a structured process around charging, battery installation, safety briefing and guided sessions.

A resort can use the ZAIHAI X1 for stable commercial operation and the X1 Pro for premium demos or advanced guests. If the resort has a performance-focused water sports center with technical staff, fuel-powered boards may also be added as an advanced activity.

Recommended resort approach:

- Start with electric boards for general guest experience.
- Add a premium electric model for advanced riders.
- Consider fuel-powered boards only if staff can manage engine and fuel process.

## Which Is Better for Rental Operators?

Rental operators should choose based on staff capability and customer profile. If customers are mostly tourists or beginners, electric boards may be easier to schedule and explain. If the rental business serves advanced riders, fuel-powered boards may create higher excitement and stronger performance appeal.

Questions for rental buyers:

- Are customers beginners or experienced riders?
- How long is each session?
- Can staff manage battery charging or fuel preparation?
- What spare parts are needed?
- What local regulations apply?
- How will damage be recorded?

A mixed fleet can work in some markets: electric boards for broader customers and fuel-powered boards for advanced packages.

## Which Is Better for Distributors?

Distributors often benefit from carrying both categories. Electric boards attract resorts, rental operators and premium leisure buyers. Fuel-powered boards attract performance riders and markets where engine-powered water sports are already familiar.

A distributor can structure the product line as:

- X1: stable electric surfboard for commercial packages.
- X1 Pro: premium electric surfboard for high-impact demos.
- P1: fuel-powered performance board.
- P1 Pro: enhanced fuel-powered model for advanced buyers.

This gives sales teams a clear comparison story. Instead of forcing every buyer into one product, the distributor can match the product to the application.

## FAQ

### Are electric surfboards better than fuel-powered surfboards?

Electric surfboards are better for many resorts, rentals and premium guest experiences because they are easier to manage and position as clean modern equipment. Fuel-powered surfboards can be better for advanced riders and performance-focused markets.

### Which type is faster?

Both categories can be fast. ZAIHAI X1 Pro lists a 0 to 61 km/h speed range. ZAIHAI P1 Pro lists up to 64 km/h. The best choice should consider rider skill, water area and business model, not only speed.

### Which type is easier for rental businesses?

Electric surfboards are often easier for beginner-friendly rentals because staff can focus on charging, battery handling and safety briefing. Fuel-powered boards may require more mechanical knowledge and fuel handling process.

### Can a distributor sell both electric and fuel-powered surfboards?

Yes. A distributor can use electric boards for resorts, rental businesses and premium leisure buyers, while offering fuel-powered boards for performance customers and advanced riders.
