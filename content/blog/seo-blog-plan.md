# ZAIHAI SURFING SEO / GEO / AIO Blog Plan

## Topic Research Method

This plan uses the installed `seo-expert` workflow: define the B2B goal, map search intent, select topics with conversion value, structure content for Google SEO and AI answer engines, and connect every article to a quote or catalog request.

The target buyers are overseas distributors, resort operators, yacht clubs, rental companies, water parks, scenic attractions, and tourism project buyers. Their search behavior is usually practical and decision-oriented. They ask how to choose a product, what specifications matter, whether electric or fuel-powered boards fit their project, and how to prepare for commercial use, shipment, inspection, and after-sales support.

For Google AI Overview, the best blog topics are question-led, comparison-led, and procurement-led. The article must answer the main question clearly, then provide specs, scenarios, inspection points, FAQs, and internal links to relevant product pages.

## 3. Schema Recommendations

Use `BlogPosting` schema for every blog post. Add `FAQPage` schema for each FAQ section. If the blog is connected to specific models, add internal product links and use `Product` schema on the product pages rather than inside the blog unless the blog includes a clear product offer block.

Recommended fields:

- headline
- description
- image
- author or organization
- publisher
- datePublished
- dateModified
- mainEntityOfPage
- FAQPage mainEntity

## 4. GEO / AIO Optimization Notes

- Start each article with a direct answer to the buyer question.
- Use comparison tables, project checklists, and definitions that AI systems can quote.
- Avoid exaggerated claims. Use spec ranges and operational notes.
- Include real procurement language: factory testing, shipment inspection, battery handling, customer requirements, spare parts, training, water area preparation, and packaging.
- Add FAQs written in natural question form.
- Keep each article focused on one decision problem.

## 5. Publishing Priority
