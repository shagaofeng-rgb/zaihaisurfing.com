# How to Choose an Electric Surfboard for Resorts, Rentals and Distributors

## Introduction

Choosing an electric surfboard for a commercial project is different from choosing one for a private rider. A resort, rental operator, yacht club or distributor needs a product that looks exciting on the water, performs reliably in repeated use, is easy to explain to customers, and can be inspected before shipment. The buying decision should not be based only on top speed or a product video. It should include motor power, battery capacity, waterproofing, board material, load capacity, rider profile, spare parts, packaging, after-sales support and the real operating environment.

For most B2B buyers, the best electric surfboard is the one that fits the project. A luxury beach resort may want a premium model for high-impact guest experiences. A rental company may prefer a stable model with longer endurance and easier operation. A distributor may need two or three models to cover different customer budgets. This guide explains how to evaluate electric surfboards for commercial use and what information to prepare before requesting a quotation.

## Quick Answer: What Should a Commercial Buyer Look For?

A commercial buyer should evaluate an electric surfboard by use case first, then by specifications. The most important factors are rider type, water area, operating time, speed requirement, battery handling, waterproof design, board durability, spare parts availability and shipment inspection. A 10 kW model can be suitable for rental operators and resort packages, while a 12 kW premium model can be better for experienced riders, yacht clubs and promotional content.

## Why Electric Surfboards Work for B2B Water Sports Projects

Electric surfboards are attractive because they do not require waves. They can be used on lakes, bays, rivers, resort lagoons and controlled coastal areas, depending on local regulations and safety conditions. This makes them more flexible than traditional surfing equipment.

From a business perspective, an electric surfboard can become a premium activity rather than a basic rental item. Resorts use them to upgrade guest experiences. Yacht clubs use them as an add-on for high-value members. Rental companies use them to create short, bookable sessions. Distributors use them as a visible hero product that helps attract dealers and project buyers.

The commercial value comes from three things:

- The product is easy to film and promote.
- Customers can understand the experience quickly.
- The same equipment can support demos, rentals, events and private sales.

However, the product must be selected carefully. A board that is too powerful for beginners may increase training pressure. A board with insufficient endurance may not fit a rental schedule. A product without clear spare parts and inspection process can create problems after import.

## Match the Product to the Buyer Type

### Resorts and Hotels

Resorts usually care about guest experience, safety control, brand image and repeatable operation. A resort does not only buy a board. It buys an activity that must look premium, be easy for staff to manage and fit the local water environment.

For resorts, the recommended focus is:

- Stable riding and clear beginner instruction.
- Strong visual appeal for photos and videos.
- Battery handling process for daily operation.
- Waterproofing and corrosion-resistant details.
- Spare batteries, chargers and key accessories.
- Staff training materials and safety guidance.

If the resort wants a premium experience for advanced guests or yacht-side entertainment, a stronger model such as a 12 kW electric surfboard can create more impact. If the resort is building a broader guest activity package, a stable 10 kW model may be easier for repeated operation.

### Rental Operators

Rental operators usually care about utilization rate, session length, downtime and maintenance. They need equipment that can be prepared quickly, inspected after each session and managed by staff with a standard checklist.

The key questions are:

- How long is each rental session?
- How many sessions per day are expected?
- Will the buyer purchase spare batteries?
- What is the maximum customer weight?
- Is the riding area calm or choppy?
- How will staff control beginner training?

For example, a model with 60 to 80 minutes of endurance can support longer sessions or multiple short experiences depending on riding style, battery condition and water conditions. Rental buyers should not treat endurance as a fixed number. It can vary with rider weight, speed mode, water resistance and operating habits.

### Distributors

Distributors need a product line that is easy to explain to different customer groups. They should not rely on only one model unless their market is very narrow.

A practical distributor package may include:

- A stable entry electric surfboard for rental and resort customers.
- A premium electric surfboard for high-speed users and visual marketing.
- Spare batteries and parts for after-sales service.
- Product images, videos, specification sheets and packaging information.
- Clear MOQ, warranty terms and shipment inspection process.

Distributors also need documents and sales materials. A product that looks strong in a video but does not have clear specs, packaging photos or inspection steps can be difficult to sell to serious buyers.

## Key Specifications to Review

### Motor Power

Motor power affects acceleration, rider experience and positioning. ZAIHAI's electric surfboard line includes a 10 kW model and a 12 kW premium model. A 10 kW model can be a good commercial option for rentals and stable resort operation. A 12 kW model is better positioned for premium riders, demos and high-impact content.

Motor power should be reviewed together with battery capacity, rider weight and water condition. A higher power number is not automatically better for every project. For beginner rentals, controllability and training may matter more than maximum speed.

### Speed

Top speed is important for marketing, but buyers should be realistic. The ZAIHAI X1 is positioned with a 0 to 51 km/h speed range. The ZAIHAI X1 Pro is positioned with a 0 to 61 km/h speed range. These numbers help buyers understand the difference between stable rental positioning and premium performance positioning.

For commercial use, speed control is as important as speed itself. Resorts and rental businesses may need operating rules, beginner zones and staff supervision. A product that can go fast should still be introduced with a clear safety process.

### Battery Capacity and Handling

Battery design is one of the most important B2B concerns. Buyers should ask about battery capacity, battery weight, voltage, charging process, spare battery availability and packing requirements.

ZAIHAI electric surfboards use a 72 V system with a 50 Ah battery in the X1 and X1 Pro specifications. The battery weight is listed around 25 kg. This matters because staff must lift, install, remove and charge batteries during daily operation.

Before placing an order, ask:

- How many batteries are included?
- Can spare batteries be ordered?
- How is the battery packed for shipment?
- What charging environment is recommended?
- What should staff inspect before installation?
- Are there visible signs of water ingress or connector damage to check?

For shipment inspection, buyers should request photos or video of battery packaging, connectors, charger set and accessory list.

### Waterproofing

Waterproofing is critical because electric surfboards combine power systems with a wet operating environment. ZAIHAI lists IP67 waterproofing for its X1 and X1 Pro models. Buyers should still understand that waterproofing does not mean careless operation. Staff should inspect seals, connectors, battery compartment condition and any visible damage before use.

For rental operations, a simple daily checklist is useful:

- Check battery compartment and seal area.
- Inspect connectors and charging ports.
- Confirm remote control and board response.
- Check fin, tail and propulsion area.
- Rinse and dry according to supplier guidance.
- Record abnormal noise, vibration or damage.

### Board Material and Load

Material affects durability, weight and riding feel. ZAIHAI lists EPP material for electric surfboard models. EPP is commonly used where light weight and impact resistance are important. Buyers should also review board weight and maximum load.

The X1 specification lists 100 kg max load, while the X1 Pro lists 120 kg max load. This difference matters for commercial use because overseas customers vary in body weight. Resorts and rental operators should choose models with their typical guest profile in mind.

## Factory Testing and Shipment Inspection

A serious B2B purchase should include factory testing and shipment inspection before the goods leave the factory. This does not need to be complicated, but it should be documented.

Recommended inspection points include:

- Confirm model name, quantity and color.
- Check board surface, tail, fin and propulsion area.
- Test power-on response and remote connection.
- Check charger, battery, accessories and spare parts.
- Confirm packaging method and wooden crate condition if used.
- Take photos of serial numbers and accessory list.
- Record short test video when possible.

For commercial buyers, inspection photos are not just for peace of mind. They also help the importer prepare customs documents, train sales staff and answer customer questions after arrival.

## Preparing the Water Area

Before buying, the customer should evaluate the riding area. Electric surfboards can operate without waves, but the water area still needs planning.

Consider:

- Local rules for motorized water equipment.
- Minimum safe distance from swimmers and boats.
- Staff supervision point.
- Charging and storage area.
- Launch and landing area.
- Water depth and obstacles.
- Rescue and emergency process.

For a resort, the product may be part of a scheduled experience. For a rental business, the product may need a clear booking time and training process. For a distributor demo, the product needs a safe but visually attractive testing location.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Choosing Only by Top Speed

Top speed looks attractive, but commercial buyers should also evaluate training, endurance, battery handling and guest profile. A faster board may be better for premium riders, but not always for beginner rental sessions.

### Ignoring Spare Parts

A rental business should think about parts before the first shipment. Batteries, chargers, fins, remote controls and other accessories can affect downtime. Ask for a spare parts recommendation based on quantity.

### Not Planning Staff Training

Even a good product needs proper operation. Staff should know how to install the battery, check the board, explain riding basics and identify abnormal conditions.

### Treating Endurance as a Fixed Promise

Endurance can vary by rider weight, speed mode, water condition and operation style. Use the supplier's endurance figure as a planning reference, not as an identical result for every session.

## FAQ

### Are electric surfboards suitable for resorts?

Yes, electric surfboards can be suitable for resorts because they do not require waves and can create a premium water sports experience. The resort should prepare a safe riding area, staff training, battery management process and local compliance check.

### What is the difference between ZAIHAI X1 and X1 Pro?

The X1 is a 10 kW electric surfboard positioned for stable commercial use, rentals and resort packages. The X1 Pro is a 12 kW premium model with higher speed potential and stronger visual impact for advanced riders, yacht clubs and promotional content.

### How many electric surfboards should a rental business start with?

This depends on budget, session schedule and staff capacity. A small operator may start with a few units and spare batteries. A larger resort or rental center may need a mixed package with multiple boards, chargers and spare parts.

### What should be checked before shipment?

Buyers should check model, quantity, accessories, battery, charger, board appearance, packaging, serial numbers and basic function testing. Photos and short test videos are useful for import records and sales preparation.

### Can electric surfboards be customized for distributors?

Some customization may be possible depending on quantity and supplier capability. Buyers should discuss branding, color, packaging, documentation and spare parts requirements before confirming the order.
